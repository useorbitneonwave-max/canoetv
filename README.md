# CanoeTV

A free IPTV player for Android phones, tablets, Android TV and Fire TV.

It comes with free, legal playlists built in:
- **All free channels**: Free-TV and iptv-org combined
- **Free-TV**: a curated list of channels that are free over the air or free online
- **iptv-org**: every country, plus lists for Canada, Trinidad & Tobago, the USA, the UK, News, Sports, Movies, Kids and Music

You can also add your own M3U playlist link.

## Download

**Fire TV / Android TV:** open the **Downloader** app and enter code **8491975**

**Phone / tablet / any browser:** https://github.com/useorbitneonwave-max/canoetv/releases/latest/download/CanoeTV.apk
(short link: aftv.news/8491975)

Both links always point to the newest version. Once it's installed, CanoeTV updates itself from **⋮ → Check for updates**.

## Using it

- **Play a channel:** tap it (or press OK on a TV remote). Tap the channel again for full screen.
- **Favourites:** tap ★, or long-press a channel.
- **Change channels in full screen:** press up/down on the remote, or channel up/down.
- **Dead channels:** they're marked red and skipped automatically. Tick **Hide offline** to hide them.

## Note on signing

The signing key (`app/canoetv.keystore`) is stored in the repo, so every build installs over the previous one. This is fine for a personal sideloaded app. Replace the key before publishing to the Play Store.
