# CanoeTV Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 14 November 2026_

CanoeTV is a TV player app for Android phones, tablets, Android TV and Fire TV. This policy explains what the app stores and what it sends over the internet.

## Summary
- No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
- Your settings stay on your device.
- CanoeTV doesn't collect, sell or share personal information.

## What is stored on your device
- Favourites and their order, renamed channels, hidden and locked categories
- Your movie My List and where you stopped in each film
- Your parental-lock PIN (if you set one)
- Recently watched channels, the last channel you watched and app settings
- Downloaded channel lists and TV guide files (a cache)

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to save a backup file. Backups don't include your PIN. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

## What the app connects to
- **Channel lists:** Free-TV (GitHub), plus any optional lists you switch on (iptv-org) or add yourself.
- **TV guide files:** epgshare01.online and any guide links named in your playlists.
- **Movies:** the Internet Archive (archive.org), for the public-domain film catalogue, posters and the films you play.
- **Streams:** when you play a channel, the video comes directly from that channel's own server.
- **App updates:** GitHub, to check for and download new versions of CanoeTV.

Like any website, these servers can see your device's IP address and basic request details. CanoeTV doesn't send them any other information about you. Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

## Children
CanoeTV isn't directed at children. A parental lock is available to restrict categories.

## Content
CanoeTV doesn't host, sell or own any channels. It plays streams that broadcasters make publicly available, and playlists you add yourself.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
